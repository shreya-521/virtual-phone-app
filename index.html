ITU & NANP Anti-Collision Rules

To prevent generating real telephone numbers owned by actual subscribers, this engine strictly restricts output to internationally designated test ranges:

  • USA & Canada (NANP): Reserved range +1 NXX 555-0100 through 555-0199.
  • United Kingdom (Ofcom): Reserved range +44 7700 900000 to 7700 900999.
  • Germany (BNetzA): Reserved range +49 1522 8880000.
